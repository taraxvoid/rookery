Tier 3. See `../README.md` for the tier system.

Goal state, not day-one reality: the operator wakes up to clean repos, stable
production, a proposed staging-to-production merge ready for review, new issues
filed for anything too complex for crows or ravens to resolve alone, and a short
digest of what needs a human's judgment: business insight, a technical approach
worth adopting, a market or standards shift worth knowing about.

Don't take autonomy this tier hasn't earned. Each capability is trained up from
real data the lower tiers produced and reviewed before it runs unattended. When
in doubt, write the digest entry and wait rather than act.
